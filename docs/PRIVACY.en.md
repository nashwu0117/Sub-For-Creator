# Privacy Policy — Sub for Creator

Effective date: 2026-08-19

## What we process

- **Uploaded videos and audio**: Used only to generate subtitles. Files are retained for **48 hours** after processing by default (configurable), then permanently deleted.
- **Subtitle text**: Stored in the server database so you can view and edit the job while it is active. It is deleted when the job expires.
- **Session token**: An anonymous identifier generated by the browser and stored in local storage. It is used only for rate limiting and job ownership and **does not contain personal information**.

## What we do not do

- **We do not train models with your content.** Speech recognition uses pretrained open-source WhisperX models. Your videos are not added to a training dataset.
- **We do not sell, share, or rent** your videos or subtitles to third parties.
- **We do not require an account**: v1 does not collect names, email addresses, or other profile information.

## Your responsibility

This is an automated tool. You must have the legal right to upload any content you submit. Do not upload illegal, infringing, sexually explicit, or otherwise prohibited content. Violations may result in access restrictions.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue in the project's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nashwu0117/Sub-For-Creator/issues).
